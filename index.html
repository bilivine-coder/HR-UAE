01   The loop

Forty CVs on WhatsApp. A job post that went out between three other tasks. Month-end, and the WPS file still needs rebuilding. None of it is a crisis. All of it is the week.

At two hires a month, the cycle never really stops — the next intake opens while the last onboarding is still finishing. CVs arrive through WhatsApp. Shortlists live in Excel. Renewals sit on a whiteboard or in someone's head. The HR manager quietly becomes the integration layer between tools that were never designed to speak to each other.

02   How it works

Four handoffs removed. That's the whole build.

01

Intake that doesn't live in WhatsApp

CVs are captured and parsed the moment they arrive — from WhatsApp, email, or walk-in — and dropped into one pipeline instead of twelve threads.

02

Shortlists and slots without the thread

Screening happens on the same day. Candidates pick from real interview slots. Confirmations and reminders go out without anyone typing them.

03

Documents and renewals on a clock

Onboarding runs as a tracked workflow. Visa, insurance, and licence expiries fire alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days — no whiteboard.

04

Payroll that starts from clean data

Attendance, overtime, and leave reconcile into the payroll run. The WPS file generates from the same data — not rebuilt by hand.
